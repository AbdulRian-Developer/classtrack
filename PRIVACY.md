# ClassTrack privacy policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

ClassTrack is an offline attendance app for class representatives.

**What the app stores.** Course names, student names and roll numbers, and attendance marks that you enter yourself. Everything is saved only on your device.

**What the app sends.** Nothing. ClassTrack has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and makes no internet requests. Data leaves your device only when you choose to share a report or a backup file through Android's share menu (for example to WhatsApp or Google Drive). Those apps' own privacy policies then apply.

**Deleting data.** Use *Settings → Erase everything* in the app, or uninstall the app.

**Children.** The app is meant for students and teachers managing class attendance. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone; all data stays on the device of the person using it.

**Contact.** For questions open an issue in this app's GitHub repository.
